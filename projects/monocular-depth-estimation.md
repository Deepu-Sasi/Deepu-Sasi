# Monocular Depth Estimation

**Intelligent Systems project, Toronto Metropolitan University**

## What it is
A deep learning model that estimates depth for every pixel from a single ordinary photo, without a stereo camera or LiDAR. Trained and evaluated on the NYU Depth V2 indoor dataset, with a written report including qualitative depth maps and error analysis.

## Skills shown
Computer vision, deep learning, model design, evaluation

## Tech
