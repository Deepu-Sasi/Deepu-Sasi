# CollisionScopeTO: Toronto Collision Hotspots

**Advanced Data Engineering project, Toronto Metropolitan University**

## What it is
A toolkit for exploring Toronto traffic collisions where people were killed or seriously injured, built on City of Toronto open data. It cleans the raw data, finds hotspots and time patterns, and presents them in an interactive desktop app.

## Skills shown
Data engineering, geospatial analysis, data visualisation, desktop app development

## Tech
Python, pandas, Matplotlib, PyQt5
