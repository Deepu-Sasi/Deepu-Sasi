# ResoBlend: Hybrid Image Quality Enhancement

**Best Paper Award, ETIS 2025**

## What it is
A hybrid super-resolution approach that combines the strengths of several generative image-enhancement models, applied to remote-sensing imagery.

## Result
About **20% improvement in image clarity** on remote-sensing data compared with the individual models.

Generative models, computer vision, research and publication
