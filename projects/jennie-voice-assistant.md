# Jennie: A Fully Local Voice Assistant

**Personal project**

## What it is
A voice assistant that listens, thinks and talks back entirely on a laptop GPU with 6GB of memory. No cloud calls, so conversations stay on the device.

## Hard problems solved
- **Barge-in:** you can interrupt Jennie mid-sentence and she stops and listens.
- **Self-hearing:** she no longer picks up and replies to her own voice.
- **Resource balancing:** speech, language and audio components share one machine without stalling each other.

## Features
Speech-to-text, an LLM for conversation, text-to-speech, long-term memory, and specialist assistants that answer from the user's own notes.

## Skills shown
Voice AI, LLM applications, real-time systems, on-device AI

## Tech
Python, local LLMs, speech recognition and synthesis
